# Conference Project Structure Plan

## Preface

This file is for storing the planned structure the conference project will be using.

## Requirements (see Week 3 .pptx)

(Side note: The target audience may not strictly be limited to the department; expect some of your readers to not understand certain concepts or technical terms.)

**1. Headers for the webpage**

**2. Title**

**3. Table of Contents**

**4. Abstract**
  - A standalone summary of all the key points from your article (150 words maximum)

**5. Main article**
  - Written in full sentences and paragraphs
  - Can include sub-headings (you do not have to use the title ‘main article’)
  -  Between 800 to 1000 words (including all figures, charts, tables, titles but excluding the abstract and reference list)

**6. Figure or table**
  - Include at least one figure or table

**7. References**
  - Include a minimum of 4 references / sources of information that are appropriate for a piece of academic writing
  - You will use hyperlinks within your article from the in-text citation to the full details in the reference list
  - When available, the reference list includes a hyperlink to the article or source of information used

Will work on the actual contents of each section in Week 4.
